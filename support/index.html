How to play

↗

Follow the arrow

Each cube can slide only in the direction of its arrow. Tap one with a clear path to send it out. A blocked cube stays in place.

◈

Look around

Drag to rotate the sculpture and pinch to zoom. Different angles can reveal an open path.

★

Clear the puzzle

Remove every cube to finish the level. There is no timer, so take your time.

A little help when you want it

✦

Hint

Highlights one cube you can remove now. It never moves the cube for you. Hint costs 5 earned coins; a free hint can be used when you cannot pay.

◎

Radar

Highlights every currently removable cube for five seconds. It does not remove anything. Radar costs 20 earned coins.

⌁

Play offline

Gameplay and local saves work without an internet connection or account. Optional anonymous analytics uses the network only if you turn it on.

Troubleshooting

A cube will not move

Check the arrow direction and rotate the puzzle to see whether another cube blocks its path. Hint or Radar can show a removable cube.

My progress looks different

Progress is stored on your device. Check that you are using the same device and have not selected Reset progress in Settings. Removing the app can remove its local save.

Sound or vibration is missing

Check the Sound and Haptics switches in the game's Settings and your device's sound settings.

The game is stuck or closes

Close and reopen the app, then check for an available update. If the problem continues, email us with your device model, iOS version, level number, and what happened. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.
